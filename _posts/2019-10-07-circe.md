Did not finish. I found it really difficult to get into a book set within the world of Greek mythology. There are gods, demi gods, nymphs, all with poorly defined powers, all somewhat immortal (maybe?), and with seemingly nothing at stake. Not my type of book, so I gave up about 25% of the way through.

## Rating

2 stars
