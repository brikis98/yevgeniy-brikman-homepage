In the 16th book of this series, Inspector Gamache and his family are in Paris, and as they are walking home after a meal, Armand's godfather is struck down by a truck. Gamache knows it is no accident, and he and Jean-Guy must work together to solve the mystery. It's an unusual Gamache book, swapping in the adorable town of Three Pines for the sprawl of Paris, but as always, Louise Penny's writing is great, there's plenty of drama, a fascinating mystery to pull you along, and a reasonably satisfying conclusion.
