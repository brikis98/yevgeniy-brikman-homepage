I found the first book in this series, _[The Spy Coast](/blog/2025/05/19/the-spy-coast/)_, to be a fun spy story with
the interesting twist of all the spies being at retirement age (a bit like _[The Thursday Murder 
Club](/book-series/thursday-murder-club)_ series). This book picks up where the previous one left off, but instead of
spy action, we mostly get a run-of-the-mill murder mystery. You could swap in almost any mystery story 
detective—Miss Marple, Poirot, the characters from _The Thursday Murder Club_—and this story would play out more or less
the same way. It's still a competent, if somewhat predictable, story, and it was an enjoyable read, but it's a shame
that it lost the uniqueness of the previous book.
