**The good**  
  
- Interesting story of vengeance and sacrifice.   
  
- Starts very slow, but picks up pace, and towards the end, is very hard to put down  
  
- Lots of great action and battles.  
  
- Interesting new fantasy world.  
  
**The not so good**  
  
- The beginning is... rough. It throws a lot at you at once, including lots of hard-to-remember names/terms, and I was close to giving up... But I stuck it out, and once I got used to it, it got much better.  
  
- The characters could be better developed if the book spent a tiny bit more time on dialog, banter, jokes, etc. and a tiny bit less time on battles.

**Rating**: 4 stars
