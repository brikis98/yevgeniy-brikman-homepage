## The good  
  
* Beautiful writing.   
* Tragic story.   
* Powerful themes: sacrificing for your children and then trying to live life through them; trying to fit in a society that fears all differences (race, gender, etc.); all that is unsaid between loved ones.   
  
## The not so good  
  
* The characters in the story were a mixed bag. They sometimes felt more like convenient plot devices or a mouthpiece for the author than real people.  
  
## Overall  
  
Well worth reading.
