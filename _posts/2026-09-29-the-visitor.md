Sergeant Amy Callan and Lieutenant Caroline Cook were both army high-flyers, both knew Jack Reacher, and both were forced to resign from the service. Now they're both dead, each found naked in her own home, in a bath full of paint. It's a really cool premise for a Reacher book, and it pulls you along, but the conclusion is a massive let down—easily the weakest ending of all the Reacher books I've read so far.

<!--more-->

I've read more than 20 books in the [Jack Reacher series](/book-series/jack-reacher), and while I know they are formulaic, the formula works really well. There's always a memorable, clever, unique opening. There's a fun mystery that pulls you along. The writing is always tight, clean, and fast-paced. Reacher is brilliant and a badass (nicknamed "Sherlock Homeless"). He sleeps with some woman along the way. He kicks some serious ass and makes a few jokes. And then, you get to the ending, which is usually a bit lame, but it's passable enough that the experience, on the whole, is an enjoyable one.

This book follows the formula to a tee, except for the ending. This one isn't just lame, it's outright _awful_. According to _[Reacher Said Nothing](/blog/2024/03/11/reacher-said-nothing/)_, Lee Child does absolutely no planning for his books in advance, writes them front to back discovering the story along the way, and never goes back to make significant edits. My guess is that with this book, he got to end, found that he had written himself into a corner, and instead of going back and fixing things, he just tossed in some utter bullshit, and submitted the manuscript.

It was the kind of dumb ending that makes you want to throw the book across the room. I groaned and I cringed. It made reading the whole book feel like a waste of time. I know that sounds dramatic, but it really was that bad. Fortunately, from the other 2 dozen Reacher books I've read, I know this one is an anamoly, and I'll continue reading this series. But do yourself a favor, and skip this one.
