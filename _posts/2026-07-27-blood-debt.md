In the 11th book of this series, Victor, our favorite hyper-paranoid professional assassin, is back, and this time, he's working for the Russian Mafia. Just as Victor is completing his final assignment, the head of the Russian Mafia is assassinated, and everyone assumes Victor did it. Victor must track down the real killer, while being hunted himself. I could've sworn one of the earlier Victor the Assassin books had a nearly identical plot, but honestly, it doesn't matter. It's just a flimsy pretense to get Victor into interesting situations. And it works. This book is full of exciting action, badass rivals, and a predictable, but reasonably satisfying ending. Onto the next book!
