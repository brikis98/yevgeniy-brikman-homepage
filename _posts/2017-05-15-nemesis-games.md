Another wonderful entry in The Expanse series. Unlike book 4, where the characters were a bit flat (all of them either superhero good or supervillian bad), this book takes you deep into the backstories of many of the main characters, making them more well-rounded, flawed, and interesting. On top of these deeply personal tales, you get a gigantic galaxy-wide plot, full of war, politics, intrigue, alien technology, and more. It's a very fun combo and the book is very hard to put down.
