In the second book of this series, the Midway star system is trying to survive on its own after former Syndicate CEOs Gwen Iceni and Artur Drakon broke away from the crumbling Syndicate Worlds and declared independence. Iceni now runs the government and Drakon commands the ground forces, but neither fully trusts the other. With the Syndicate still eager to reclaim the system and threats closing in from beyond its borders, they have to hold Midway together and defend it with only a small, inexperienced flotilla. After reading [14 books in the Lost Fleet series](/book-series/lost-fleet), I can safely say I'm a fan of Jack Campbell's writing. This series isn't _quite_ as strong as the Lost Fleet series, but it's still highly entertaining: great space battles, devious politics and strategy, cringe-worthy romance. On to the next one!
