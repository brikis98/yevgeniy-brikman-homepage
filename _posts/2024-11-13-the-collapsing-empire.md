A decent Sci Fi novel. The strength is definitely the characters, especially Kiva and Nadashe; the dialog can be witty, wicked, and fun; the plot is intriguing, but not especially original; and the book feels a bit short, with the storyline only moving along a small amount before the book ends. But overall, it's entertaining, and I'm curious what happens in the next book.
