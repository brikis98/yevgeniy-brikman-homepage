## The good  
  
- Kenzie and Gennaro are back.   
  
- More interesting private detective work.  
  
- The Boston setting is back, which is Lehane's specialty.  
  
- Some decent suspense, action, etc.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- At times, unnecessarily violent / gory.   
  
- A bit of an odd ending. It mostly works, but I'm curious how it'll carry over into the rest of this series.
