**The good**  
  
- Fun characters. Lila is especially fun.  
  
- Fascinating new world.  
  
- Good writing and pacing.  
  
- Fun action.  
  
**The not so good**  
  
- The magical system is poorly defined, so you get a bit of deus ex machina.

**Rating**: 4 stars
