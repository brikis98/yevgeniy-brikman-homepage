## The good

- An interesting protagonist: a lawyer who is a former conman (is there a difference?).
- Exciting, fast-paced story that grabs you from the first page.
- Clever twists and turns, and some good competence porn.
- A reasonably satisfying conclusion.

<!--more-->

## The not so good

- Some of the twists and turns are improbable, to say the least. But that's pretty typical for this sort of novel.
