Did not finish. Heard a lot of good things about this, and I like the art style, but the content is not my type of book. Some kind of strange world with winged people, horned people, people with TVs for heads, giant turtles, spider-women, and a whole bunch of other weirdness... Mixed with so-so writing... I just couldn't get into it, and didn't even finish this first volume.
