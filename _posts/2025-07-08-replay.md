I was a huge fan of [_The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August_](/blog/2017/02/22/the-first-fifteen-lives-of-harry-august/), and this book, which has a similar time loop premise, but came out ~15 years earlier, is perhaps even better. Strong characters, great writing, compelling ideas, and a solid conclusion.
