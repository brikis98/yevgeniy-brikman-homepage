## The good  
  
- Intriguing central mystery.  
  
- Suspenseful, grippy plot.  
  
- Interesting characters.   
  
## The not so good  
  
- I was a bit mixed on the conclusion. Makes sense in some ways, and a bit disappointing in others.

## Rating

4.5 stars
