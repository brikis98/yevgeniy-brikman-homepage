## The good

- A decent whodunit mystery.
- I always enjoy locked-room style mysteries; this one takes place aboard a ship.
- A few good twists and turns.
- A reasonably satisfying conclusion.

<!--more-->

## The not so good

- The protagonist can be a bit whiny and annoying.
