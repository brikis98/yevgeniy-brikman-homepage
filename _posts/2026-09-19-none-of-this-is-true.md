While celebrating her 45th birthday at her local pub, popular podcaster Alix Summers meets an unassuming woman named Josie Fair, who, it turns out, is celebrating her 45th birthday that very same day. Eventually, Alix decides to interview Josie on her podcast, and as they chat, she starts to realize that Josie has been hiding some very dark secrets. The result is a moderately entertaining, though fairly predictable, thriller and mystery story.

<!--more-->

Although it's a thriller and mystery story, the first thought that comes to mind when I think about this book is "creepy." The book does a great job of gradually escalating the creepy and crazy factor, which makes it hard to put down. The characters are well-defined, the plot is well-paced, and it's largely an interesting, albeit disturbing story.

The one flaw is that if you take some time to think about it, you can probably guess the big twist. I won't put any spoilers here, but it doesn't take a genius to pick up on it. With some small changes, the twist could have been much more impactful, but alas, it ends up being more or less exactly what you expect.

Still, it's a reasonably interesting read, and a fairly unique story overall.
