If you like xkcd, you'll probably like this book. There's lots of physics, nerd humor, computer humor, stick figure drawings, math, and so on.   
  
Some of the questions/answers are hilarious and memorable: I particularly liked the ones about throwing a baseball at relativistic speeds; swimming in a pool full of spent nuclear fuel; a wall that has all the elements from the periodic table; what would happen if you had 1 mole (6.022×10^23) of moles (the furry animals); what orbital speed really means; what would happen if we drained all the earth's oceans through a giant hole; and what would happen if we shot a bullet that was the density of a neutron star. But some of the other questions/answers were weird, or forgettable, or a bit confusing.   
  
Overall, I find it slightly educational, moderately funny, and largely entertaining.

## Rating

4 stars
