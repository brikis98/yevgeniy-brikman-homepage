An amusing, sometimes weird, occasionally interesting, and almost largely forgettable memoir of Nick Offerman. The most interesting parts to me were his descriptions of how casting in the movie industry really works—the endless auditions, rejections, egos, and so on. There was also plenty of discussion of wood working, eating meat, drugs and alcohol, the problems with religion, and random stories from Offerman's life.
