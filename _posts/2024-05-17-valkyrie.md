## The good  
  
- The first half progresses the plot nicely, with humanity trying to find a way to survive amidst more advanced alien species.  
  
- Near the very end, some major reveals about the larger plot line with the Elders. Finally!  
  
- The typical clever problem solving and exciting action you expect from the series.  
  
- The typical cheesy humor and banter between Joe and Skippy that you expect from the series.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- The typical adolescent, eye-rolling humor you expect from the series.  
  
- The typical "I can't believe I was outsmarted by a dirty monkey" repetition you expect from the series.  
  
- Much of the second half focuses on a random subplot of trying to save a small group of humans. This felt like a jarring change to the pacing of the book; almost as if two books were glued together.
