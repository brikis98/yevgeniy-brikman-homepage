When a Commonwealth patrol makes first contact with an unknown alien race, the aliens open fire without warning and destroy every ship but one. Commander Pheylan Cavanagh, son of the powerful former politician Lord Stewart Cavanagh, survives the ambush only to be taken prisoner by the aliens. While the Commonwealth scrambles to prepare for a war against an enemy it knows almost nothing about, the elder Cavanagh decides to take matters into his own hands and get his son back. This creates the backdrop for an interesting space opera and first contact story, with great world building and unique aliens, but boring human characters.

<!--more-->

The entire Cavanagh family consists of a bunch of tropes. Pheylan Cavanagh is the classic tough, self-sacrificing hero. Lord Steward Cavanagh is the classic gray-haired politician who is smarter than everyone and really knows how to wheel and deal. Melinda Cavanagh is the doctor, who is tougher than she looks, and cares for all the men. And so on.

The good news is that the alien characters are actually a lot more interesting. Though physically they are still a little too similar to humanoid lizards, they act in strange and seemingly incomprehensible ways, which seems right for a totally alien species. 

More generally, the book has good world building, and a lot of mysteries that keep you reading. Why did the aliens perform an unprovoked attack on humanity? Why are they holding Pheylan captive? What are the mysterious structures they build near his prison? It's just enough to keep me curious enough to go on to the next book in the series.
