DNF.

Ken Liu's _[The Paper Menagerie and Other Stories](/blog/2025/09/29/paper-menagerie-and-other-stories/)_ is one of my
favorite short-story collections ([S tier](/blog/2026/01/23/the-sci-fi-standalone-book-tier-list/)!), so I was
surprised that this book didn't work for me at all. I didn't like the writing (too much tell, not enough show), the
characters felt wooden, the dialog was stilted, and the plot was boring. I gave up about 20% of the way through.
