The four elite 60-something female assassins, Billie, Helen, Mary Alice, and Natalie, are back! And once again, there
is someone hunting them, so these badass ladies must band together one more time to stay alive, kick some ass, and make
lots of penis and vagina jokes. 

<!--more-->

It's the same formula from the first book, but it worked really well there, and it still works really well here. The
characters are great. The action scenes are thrilling. There's a decent central mystery and a few plot twists. And,
most importantly, the book doesn't take itself too seriously. I found myself laughing and snickering often.

I hope there will be a third one!
