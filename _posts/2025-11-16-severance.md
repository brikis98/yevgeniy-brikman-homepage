A post-apocalyptic story where much of civilization gets wiped out by a plague that turns people into drones that get
stuck in endless loops, and a woman named Candace who almost doesn't notice because she's too busy working a boring 
desk job where she often feels like a drone stuck in an endless loop. It's a clever idea, but unfortunately, the book 
doesn't do much with it. 

<!--more-->

The story alternates between the present, where the book somehow manages to make surviving the apocalypse
sound a bit boring, and the past, where the story walks you through Candace's unsatisfying life. Somehow, amidst a 
chaotic and occasionally fascinating background (especially as Candace wanders the streets of an abandoned NYC), the
story somehow manages to be a bit dull.
