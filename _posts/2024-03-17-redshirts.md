## The good  
  
- A fun parody of _Star Trek_ that clearly loves the source material. In some ways, it feels like _Galaxy Question_, but in book form.  
  
- Occasionally genuinely funny.  
  
- Fun characters and good dialog.  
  
- Surprisingly touching ending (especially the epilogues).  
  
## The not so good  
  
- A little predictable.  
  
- Sometimes funny, but sometimes just silly.  
  
- On the whole, _Galaxy Quest_ is a more thorough and funnier parody.
