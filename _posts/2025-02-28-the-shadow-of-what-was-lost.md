## The good  
  
- Interesting characters.  
  
- Great world building.  
  
- Some good action scenes.   
  
- A number of mysteries to keep you reading.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- At times, there are a few too many mysteries tossed in all at once that you lose track of what you're trying to resolve.  
  
- While most of the action scenes are decent, a lot of the story builds up to one major action scene that ultimately turns out to be quite disappointing.  
  
- While the magic system seems to have some rules and restrictions, it ultimately adapts to whatever the plot needs, and sometimes feels like deus ex machina.

## Rating

4 stars
