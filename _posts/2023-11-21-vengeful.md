I though the first book in this series, _Vicious_, was great; I think I liked this second book even better. The characters are great, both the ones coming back from the first book and the new ones introduced in this book, the superpowers are creative and used in fun ways, the action is great, the plot hums along nicely, and the ending is satisfying. I'd love to see another book in this series!

**Rating**: 5 stars
