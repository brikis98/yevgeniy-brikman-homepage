A delightful children's story I never got to read as a child, but was able to now. Cute characters, hilarious "gobblefunk" language (delumptious human beans, sloshfunking snozzcumbers, and, of course, earbursting whizzpoppers), funny story, and lovely illustrations.

## Rating

4 stars
