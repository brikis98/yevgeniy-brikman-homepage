At a wedding, Elizabeth meets Nick, who asks for her help in trying to figure out who is trying to kill him. Shortly
after, Nick disappears, and the Thursday Murder Club has a new mystery to solve. And it's a good one. In fact, if
anything, I think Richard Osman is getting better, as this is one of the best books in the series.

<!--more-->

All the wonderful characters from the previous book are back. The sad themes from the previous book are back
too, and handled with grace. As always, it's a lovely mix of humor, tragedy, mystery solving, friendship, enemies,
and love. I can't wait for the next one!
