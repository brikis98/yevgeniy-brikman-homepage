**The good**  
  
- Interesting idea for a mysterious creature that evolves in an isolated part of the world.  
  
- A couple of moderately interesting characters, especially the main character, Pendergast.  
  
- A few moderately entertaining action scenes.  
  
**The not so good**  
  
- Despite the attempt at putting a scientific spin on the creature, it still largely comes off as a magical monster.  
  
- We only get to see the main character, Pendergast, for a tiny portion of the book. Feels like a wasted opportunity.

**Rating**: 3 stars
