## The good  
  
- This one is a page turner. There's fast-paced action and a fun central mystery that makes it hard to put down.  
  
- An interesting look at the airline and TV journalism industries.  
  
- A tight plot with a reasonably satisfying conclusion.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- The characters are OK, but no one is particularly interesting.
