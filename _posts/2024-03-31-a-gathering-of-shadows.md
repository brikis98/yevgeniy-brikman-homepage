## The good  
  
- Great characters. Especially Lila. And since most of them are returning characters from the first book, instead of spending time on basic introductions, the book gets to go deeper.  
  
- More good world building. The magical tournament was really fun.  
  
- Good writing and pacing.  
  
- Fun action.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- The magical system remains poorly defined, so more deus ex machina.  
  
- Minor spoiler: the main villain spends the entire book brooding in the background, doing nothing, until the very final few chapters, and then the book ends.
