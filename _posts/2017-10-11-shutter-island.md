A fun psychological thriller/suspense novel. Lots of twists and turns, interesting characters, and a satisfying conclusion. I was particularly impressed by the writing, which was surprisingly impactful. When the main character is feeling happy, you feel happy; when they're depressed, you feel depressed; and when the insane asylum starts to affect the main character and make him paranoid and crazy, you feel a little paranoid and crazy yourself. Great stuff.
