## The good  
  
- Starts out with an intriguing premise that draws you in.  
  
- As is the case with all the Jack Reacher books, this one is a page turner that can be hard to put down.  
  
- Occasionally funny dialog.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- Slows down towards the end.  
  
- The ending is a bit of a let down.   
  
- The fights are repetitive and not particularly interesting.
