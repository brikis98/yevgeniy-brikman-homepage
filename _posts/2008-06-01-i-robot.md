I, Robot was my first exposure to Asimov and I loved it. The book is essentially a handful of short stories that discuss AI and the interaction of humans, robots and morality. I found myself smiling a lot while reading the book as I pondered the various logical and philosophical conundrums created by the possibility of intelligent machines. It's a very quick read and guaranteed to get you thinking.
