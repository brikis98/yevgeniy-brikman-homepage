A highly entertaining read.   
  
## The good  
  
- Tight, exciting, fast paced writing. This book is very hard to put down.   
  
- Fun characters and witty, cutting dialog.   
  
- Intriguing mystery and a clever, tough detective to solve it.   
  
## The not so good  
  
- The explanation for the mystery is convoluted and a bit silly.   
  
- The final action sequence is a little implausible.
