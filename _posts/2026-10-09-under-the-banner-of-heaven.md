In 1984, brothers Ron and Dan Lafferty, two Mormon fundamentalists, murder their sister-in-law and her baby daughter, insisting they received a revelation from God commanding them to do it. Starting from this "divinely inspired" crime, the book looks at the history of Mormon fundamentalists, who still practice polygamy, treat women and children as property of the priesthood, and force girls as young as 12 to marry men 2-3 times their age. It's a fascinating and disturbing look into Mormonism, showing both how utterly insane it is as a religion, and how all other religions are just as crazy—it's just that most of them have thousands of years to obscure their origin stories, so we accept them as normal.

<!--more-->

Some of the (mostly horrifying) things I learned from this book:

## Mormon fundamentalists and polygamy

- Polygamy ("celestial marriage") is a core part of the sacred texts of Mormonism. 
- Of course, polygamy is illegal in the US, and after enough legal pressure, the main Mormon church banned polygamy. 
- However, a sect called the Fundamentalist Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints (FLDS) broke away from the Mormon church, and they continue to practice polygamy. 
- They represent a small percentage of the entire church (though they still have over 100,000 members), and they are mostly fundamentalists, and mostly nuts.

## How they work around the law banning polygamy

- Legally, they only marry the first wife. 
- The others they marry in private ceremonies hidden from the government.  
- That allows all the other wives to register as "single mothers" and qualify for welfare. 
- As a result, Colorado City, where many of these Mormon fundamentalists live, has roughly 33% of the population on food stamps, which is 7x the Arizona statewide average of 4.7%.
- One of the beliefs of these fundamentalists is that it is their duty to bleed the government dry.

## The horrors of arranged marriages, child marriages, and women and children being property of the priesthood

- Members of the FLDS do not choose their spouses. Instead, the prophet (the leader of the FLDS) assigns wives to men through a system called "placement."
- This included forcing girls as young as _12_ (middle schoolers!) to marry men 2-3 times their age, who would then rape and impregnate them.
- Some of the men have 40+ wives and over 100 children. 
- They regularly marry their step daughters.
- All wives and children are property of the priesthood and can be taken away from a man at any time and given to another man.

## Other crazy beliefs

- Enforced dress code: 

    - Women are required to wear long, ankle-length pioneer-style prairie dresses with high necklines and long sleeves, and they are strictly forbidden from cutting their hair, wearing makeup, or showing skin. 
    - Men are required to wear plain, button-down shirts and slacks.

- They ban: 

    - Talking to anyone outside the religion
    - Secular books
    - Toys
    - Television
    - The Internet
    - Going to public schools

- They believe:

    - The earth is 7 thousand years old
    - Man has _not_ landed on the moon

## The background of Mormonism

- Mormonism was started by Joseph Smith, a known conman, with a long history of legal issues and easily provable lies.
- He claimed that an angel visited him, led him to a set of inscribed golden plates, and that he translated those into _The Book of Mormon_.
- It has insane claims:

    - Jesus visited North America
    - Native Americans are descendants of people from the Middle East (though, of course, DNS tests conclusively proves this is false)
    - It has all sorts of anachronisms, such as horses and wheels in North America, long before either was there

- That said, the claims of all religious books are just as insane. It's just that this is a recent religion, so we could witness it's formation more closely, and know how false it is.
