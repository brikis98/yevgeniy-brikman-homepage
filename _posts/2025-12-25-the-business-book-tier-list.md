I've read a lot of business books in my life. Some profoundly shaped how I think; others were a waste of time. Quite
a few became a big part of how we built [Gruntwork](https://www.gruntwork.io/), which will soon turn 10 years old.
I share all my [book reviews on my 
blog](/blog/?ybrikman_blog%5BrefinementList%5D%5BType%5D%5B0%5D=Review%3A%20Nonfiction&ybrikman_blog%5BrefinementList%5D%5BType%5D%5B1%5D=Review%3A%20Fiction),
but as I've reviewed over 1,000 books now, I've had a number of people ask me for advice on where to get started. To
try to answer this question, I've decided to throw together a few tier lists, where I take all the books I've read 
within some genre, and I arrange them in tiers from best to worst.

In this blog post, I've put together a tier list for business books. More specifically, **I've arranged the books from 
most to least useful to me, as I navigated the challenges of building my own business.** This isn't a ranking of 
whether these books are objectively "good" or "bad," but merely whether, in my day-to-day work, the insights in these
books made me better at my job.
