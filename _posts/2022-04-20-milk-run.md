## The good  
  
- Fun new universe. It feels like a real place, with its own politics, economics, and hardships, rather than some futuristic sci-fi utopia.  
  
- Interesting characters. Most of them feel like real, fully-fleshed out people.   
  
- Some neat sci fi ideas.  
  
- A little bit of deduction work.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- The deduction work isn't particularly interesting and doesn't give the reader a chance to really participate.  
  
- Not much happens in the plot. Although there is some murder and mystery in the background, the plot primarily focuses on a few deliveries across space, a bit like reading about an outer space version of FedEx.
