Reasonably entertaining whodunnit. There are some decent twists in the story, and while the ending is guessable part way through, the book still does a good job of keeping you reading and engaged. The biggest downside is that all the female characters seem to exclusively focus on three things: (a) obsessing over men, (b) obsessing over babies, (c) being depressed. Really? They have no hobbies? No career goals? No friends? No family?
