## The good  
  
- Zoe is a very fun character.  
  
- Zoe's relationship with her parents, her friends, and Hickory and Dickory is genuinely touching. There are some very emotional scenes in this story that hit hard.  
  
- It's interesting to see the previous book, _The Last Colony_, retold from a different viewpoint.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- A little bit cheesy / young adult at times.  
  
- I'm not sure there's quite enough here for a totally standalone book. I wonder if the beautiful relationships in this story, had they been added to _The Last Colony_, wouldn't have made for a single, spectacular book.

## Rating

4 stars
