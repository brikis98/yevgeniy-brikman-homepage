One winter evening, Elín S. Jónsdóttir, a bestselling crime author, goes missing without a trace. With no clues to go on, it's up to Helgi, a young detective, to solve the case. It's a multi-layered story, that weaves together multiple narratives, including a mysterious bank robbery that happened 50 years prior, Helgi's violent and creepy ex-girlfriend, some sort of secret interview with a journalist, and more. Ultimately, it's a competent, if not especially memorable mystery story, with decent but slightly bland characters, and a few minor twists.
