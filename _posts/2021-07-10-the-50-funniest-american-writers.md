I really hope these aren't the 50 funniest American writers, or the country is in real trouble. Of the 50 stories in here, I'd say that roughly:   
  
- 5 are actually funny  
- 5 are not funny, but entertaining and readable  
- 40 are a mix of boring, bizarre, decidedly unfunny, or so bad I couldn't even finish them  
  
So as far as hit or miss ratios go, this book doesn't fare well.

## Rating

2 stars
