## The good

- All the good parts of the [previous book](/blog/2025/09/11/super-powereds-year-1/): fun premise, great characters, creative superpowers, and fun action scenes.
- Since the premise and characters are already familiar, this second book is able to dive right into the fun. 
- The basic premise of superheroes training in a university has a wonderful advantage: we get to see the heroes grow and improve, which is satisfying to see.
- Reveals more of the underlying mystery.
- Things are starting to get more serious and grown-up.

<!--more-->

## The not so good

- Still some of the same boring college themes from the previous book, but not as bad.

## Overall

An excellent second entry in the series. Onto [book 3](/blog/2025/09/25/super-powereds-year-3/)!
