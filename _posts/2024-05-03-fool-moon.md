## The good  
  
- This book introduces werewolves—multiple types of werewolves, in fact—into the series, which is interesting.  
  
- The combo of noir detective mixed with fantasy monster battles is still fun.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- The book leaned way too hard into making the werewolves as violent and hyper sexualized and violently hyper sexualized as possible. It felt gross and cartoony.  
  
- The first book seemed a bit funnier and snarkier than this one. This one took itself a bit too seriously.  
  
- The "with great power comes great responsibility" angle felt cheesy.
