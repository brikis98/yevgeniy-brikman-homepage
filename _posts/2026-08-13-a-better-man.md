In the 15th book of this series, Armand Gamache returns to the Sûreté du Québec after his suspension. As record spring floods threaten the province, Gamache faces a storm of public criticism on social media. Then a distraught father, Homer Godin, asks for help finding his missing daughter, Vivienne. The melodrama in this one is turned up to 11. It's _almost_ too much. But Louise Penny is a terrific writer, and somehow, she makes it work, and gives us another good murder mystery.
