Another excellent entry in the series. You get delightful characters and a fun mystery, just like the previous stories, 
but this story also adds a more serious and tragic tone. The book handles this serious topic in a beautiful and 
dignified manner, so the tragedy ends up as a beautiful contrast to the comedy. A powerful and entertaining read.
