## The good  
  
- Reveals some of the origin story of the silos. It's a bit whacky, but mostly holds together.  
  
- A few good twists and turns. Eventually, it connects to the events of the first book of the series in a mostly satisfying way.  
  
- Some interesting characters.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- The story jumps around and through time quite a bit, so it's hard to get attached to the characters.

**Rating**: 4 stars
