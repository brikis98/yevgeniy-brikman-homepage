**The good**  
  
- The core premise is intriguing: what happens when almost everyone on earth goes blind?  
  
- A number of interesting concepts: post-apocalyptic society; walking, carnivorous plants called Triffids; the cause of the blindness (which I'll leave out to avoid spoilers); etc.  
  
- Decent pacing and action.  
  
**The not so good**  
  
- The characters are mostly flat tropes: the heroic male protagonist; the damsel in distress; etc.

**Rating**: 3.5 stars
