**The good**  
  
- Intriguing premise of "snapshots" of parts of the world that are reproduced to help investigate crimes. Good exploration of the implications of such a technology on privacy, relationships, self-awareness, crime, and so on.  
  
- Interesting plot with a reasonably satisfying conclusion.  
  
- Interesting characters, but due to the shorty story nature, they don't get developed too much.  
  
**The not so good**  
  
- The final twist is fairly guessable.  
  
- It's a shame it's a short story; it would've been interesting to see a full-length story in this world.

**Rating**: 4 stars
