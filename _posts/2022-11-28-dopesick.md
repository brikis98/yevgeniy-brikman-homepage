## The good  
  
- Helps to raise an important issue to public consciousness.  
  
- I had no idea how bad the opioid epidemic had gotten: the #1 cause of death for Americans under 50! Yikes.  
  
- A damning picture of the drug companies involved; a sad tale of the thousands (millions?) of people who got hurt by it.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- It's mostly a journalistic writing style, which is fine for a newspaper length article, but gets a bit boring for something the length of a book. There are glimpses here and there into the lives of specific people affected by opioids, but nothing that adds up to a story.
