Another quick and entertaining read in the series, although by now, it's getting a bit repetitive. This book didn't introduce anything new over the previous ones and felt a bit forgettable. Even a few days later, I'm struggling to remember exactly what happened; I know there were more space battles, more strategy & tactics, more worrying over leadership, and more gag-worthy romance... But that's about as much as I can say. I'm hoping the series wraps up soon, and with more of a bang.
