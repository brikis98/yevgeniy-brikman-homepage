This is the second book in the Grimm's War Series. One of my complaints with the first book was that it felt like a
generic space military opera, and wasn't particularly memorable. This book is worse. I'm writing this review about 2
months after reading the book, _and I can't remember much of anything_. I pulled up the plot summary online, and it
still doesn't ring a bell. I have a few vague memories of mildly interesting space battles, but other than that,
this is such a derivative book, that nothing sticks. I will not be reading the rest of the series.
