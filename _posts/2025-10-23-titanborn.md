## The good

- An interesting futuristic setting where humanity has expanded across our solar system, but instead of joining 
  together and living harmoniously (ala _Star Trek_), it's messy, gritty, and violent (a la _[The 
  Expanse](/book-series/expanse)_).
- A few interesting characters.
- A handful of fun fight scenes.

<!--more-->

## The not so good

- The setting is interesting, but it feels underdeveloped. The world-building in _The Expanse_ was better.
- The "twist" is predictable.
