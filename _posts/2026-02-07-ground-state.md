Wow, book 19. Hard to believe I've followed Joe and Skippy this far into the series. And all you really need to know is
I'm very much ready for book 20!

<!--more-->

This book follows the formula of the others. Joe and Skippy run into a problem that seems hopeless. Skippy says it's
impossible. Joe tries to solve it, and Skippy calls him a dumb monkey. Somehow, Uhtavio Scorandum gets involved, and 
does some hilarious and sketchy stuff. Eventually, Joe finds a way to do the impossible. Skippy says he hates his life. 
With the problem solved, everyone is happy... until they discover the unintended consequences of their actions.

What can I say? The formula works. Can't wait for the next one!
