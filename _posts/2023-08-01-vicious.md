## The good  
  
- Cool new (and dark) concept for superhero origin stories.  
  
- Fun characters with interesting (and dark) abilities.   
  
- Tightly paced, exciting, hard to put down.  
  
## The not so good  
  
- Everyone seems way to accepting of super powers, murders, etc. They all just roll with it, rather than freaking out like a proper person.

## Rating

5 stars
