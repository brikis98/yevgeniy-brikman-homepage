Tova Sullivan is a widow who works the night cleaning shift at the Sowell Bay Aquarium, keeping busy to cope with the loss of her husband and with the mysterious disappearance of her eighteen-year-old son, Erik. She meets Cameron, a 30-year-old drifter, and Marcellus, a giant Pacific octopus who occasionally manages to escape from his tank, and the three of them form an unlikely friendship. It's a cute, feel-good story, with charming characters, and while it can be a bit cheesy at times, it ultimately makes for a pleasant read.

<!--more-->

Marcellus, the octopus, steals the show. I'll avoid any spoilers, other than to say that that just about every scene he's in is great. Tova is a good character too, though the way she acts sometimes feels less realistic than the octopus. Cameron is OK at times, annoying at other times. 

The plot feels a bit contrived, but it works. It reminds me of Fredrik Backman books such as _[Anxious People](/blog/2023/09/07/anxious-people/)_ and _[A Man Called Otto](m/blog/2023/08/16/a-man-called-otto/)_: you'll roll your eyes now and then, but most of the time, you just find yourself smiling.
