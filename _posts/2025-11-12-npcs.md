What if the worlds you imagined while playing Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) turned out to be real, and the Non-Player 
Characters (NPCs) that are in the background of those worlds came to life, and led their own adventures? 

<!--more-->

It's an interesting premise, but for the most part, it plays out as a fairly standard fantasy tale, with a group of
adventurers (a paladin, a rogue, a barbarian, and a wizard) traveling the land, and interacting with, and sometimes
fighting with, goblins, demons, elves, and orcs. Drew Hayes' writing is _just_ good enough where I'm willing to try
out the next book in the series, but so far, this one doesn't compare to his [Super 
Powereds](/book-series/super-powereds) or [Villain's Code](/book-series/villains-code) series.
