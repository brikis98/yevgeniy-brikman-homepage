Did not finish.   
  
I got about 1/5th of the way into the book and realized (a) I had absolutely no idea what was going on and (b) I was tuning in and out from boredom. Incomprehensible protagonist, weird city that's somewhat post apocalyptic but not really, random sex scenes with strangers, no plot line to follow, random tangents into flowery prose... Pass.
