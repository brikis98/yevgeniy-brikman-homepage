In the 2nd book of this series, Adrian is still surviving the zombie apocalypse on his own (well, him and his cat), recording his days and his struggles in his diary as the world around him falls apart. But as the title suggests, he comes across other survivors, which ends his isolation, but starts up a series of new problems. It's a satisfying second entry in the series that mostly follows the formula of the first one, while gradually increasing the stakes, and introducing some fun new characters. I'm looking forward to the 3rd entry!
