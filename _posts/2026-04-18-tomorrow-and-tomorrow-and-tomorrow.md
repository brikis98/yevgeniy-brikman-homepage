The story of two friends, Sam Masur and Sadie Green, who played video games together as children, and then started
a company to create video games together as adults. It's a story about love: the love between friends and the love of
video games. Although the story can be a bit cheesy at times, and the plot has a few pacing issues, overall, it's
well-written, has compelling characters, and is a nice ode to gaming.
