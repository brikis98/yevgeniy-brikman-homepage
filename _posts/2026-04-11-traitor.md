First, Victor is ambushed by a team of hitmen. He survives, only to immediately be arrested for a murder he didn't
commit. Someone has betrayed him, and he spends the rest of the story trying to figure out who that is, leaving
countless bodies in his wake. Unlike [the previous book in this series](/blog/2026/03/23/a-quiet-man/), where Victor
did a somewhat unsuccessful impersonation of Jack Reacher, this book returns him back to his hitman roots, and is a
lot more entertaining.

<!--more-->

There isn't much new here, but if you're reading the 10th book in the series, that shouldn't be much of a
disappointment. Victor is back, and as cold, calculating, and lethal as ever. It's fun competence porn, with some good
action scenes, plot twists, and a reasonably satisfying ending. On to the next one!
