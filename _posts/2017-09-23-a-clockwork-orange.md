Disturbing, bizarre, provocative, dark, silly, violent, and memorable. This is definitely one of the more unique books (and movies) you'll find. Not for the faint of heart, and not always enjoyable, but definitely worth reading/seeing.   
  
Side note: I found the use of Russian as hooligan slang very odd, but also quite memorable.
