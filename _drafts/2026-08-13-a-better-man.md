In the 15th book of this series, Armand Gamache returns to the Sûreté du Québec after his suspension, demoted to co-head of the homicide department alongside his son-in-law, Jean-Guy Beauvoir, who is about to leave for a new job in Paris. As record spring floods threaten the province, Gamache faces a storm of public criticism on social media. Then a distraught father, Homer Godin, asks for help finding his missing daughter, Vivienne.
