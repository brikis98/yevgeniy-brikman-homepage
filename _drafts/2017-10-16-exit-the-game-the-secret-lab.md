You and your fellow players have signed up as subjects for a medical study, but when you arrive at the lab right on time, no one else seems to be there. Then steam starts rising from one of the test tubes, everyone gets dizzy and passes out, and when you wake up, the door is locked and there's no obvious way out. All you have to work with is a notebook and a mysterious spinning code dial, and you'll need to crack codes, solve puzzles, and collect objects to escape bit by bit.
