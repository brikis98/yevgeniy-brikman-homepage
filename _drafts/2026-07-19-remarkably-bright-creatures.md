Tova Sullivan is a widow who works the night cleaning shift at the Sowell Bay Aquarium, keeping busy to cope with the loss of her husband and with the mysterious disappearance of her eighteen-year-old son, Erik, who vanished on a boat more than thirty years ago. One night, she meets Marcellus, a giant Pacific octopus who sees everything that happens in the aquarium but has no interest in helping his human captors. As an unlikely friendship forms between them, Marcellus pieces together what really happened to Erik, and he must find a way to reveal the truth to Tova before his time runs out.
