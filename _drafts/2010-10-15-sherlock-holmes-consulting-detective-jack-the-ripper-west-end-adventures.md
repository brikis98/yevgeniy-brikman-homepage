It's 1888 in Victorian London, and a series of brutal murders in Whitechapel has the city in a panic, with victims Mary Ann Nichols, Annie Chapman, Elizabeth Stride, Catherine Eddowes, and Mary Jane Kelly turning up one after another. Working alongside Sherlock Holmes, you comb through a London Directory, a map of the city, and archives of the day's newspapers, deciding which addresses to visit and which leads to follow, as you try to piece together who is behind the Jack the Ripper killings. Then, in six more cases set around the West End, you take on everything from the strange case of Dr. Goldfire and the death of a Transylvanian count to a royal huggermugger at the Savage Club, and even the murder of Sherlock Holmes himself.
