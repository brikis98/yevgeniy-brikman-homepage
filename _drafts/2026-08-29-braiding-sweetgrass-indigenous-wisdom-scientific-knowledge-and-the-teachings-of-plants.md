Robin Wall Kimmerer, a botanist and enrolled member of the Citizen Potawatomi Nation, weaves together scientific knowledge and Indigenous teachings to explore our relationship with plants and the natural world. Through a series of essays drawing on her life as a scientist, a mother, and a Potawatomi woman, she argues that we should see the earth not as a collection of resources to exploit, but as a web of gifts that calls for gratitude and reciprocity.
