Discworld is a flat world that rides through space on the backs of four giant elephants, which in turn stand on the shell of an enormous turtle named Great A'Tuin. Twoflower, the Disc's first-ever tourist, arrives in the city of Ankh-Morpork carrying a fortune in gold and a walking, many-legged chest called the Luggage. Rincewind, an inept and cowardly failed wizard, ends up as his reluctant guide, tasked with keeping this wide-eyed and hopelessly naive visitor alive.
