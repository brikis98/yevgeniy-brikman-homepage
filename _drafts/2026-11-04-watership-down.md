Fiver, a small and anxious young rabbit, has a terrible vision that something awful is about to happen to his home, Sandleford Warren. When the warren's leaders refuse to listen, Fiver and his brother Hazel gather a small band of rabbits and flee into the English countryside, facing predators, men, and hostile warrens as they search for a new home on the distant hills of Watership Down.
