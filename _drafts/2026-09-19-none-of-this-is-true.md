While celebrating her 45th birthday at her local pub, popular podcaster Alix Summers meets an unassuming woman named Josie Fair, who, it turns out, is celebrating her 45th birthday that very same day. A few days later, Josie turns up outside Alix's children's school, says she's been listening to Alix's podcasts, and suggests she'd make an interesting subject, as she is on the cusp of great changes in her life. Alix finds Josie unsettling, but she can't resist the temptation to keep recording, even as she starts to realize that Josie has been hiding some very dark secrets, and that Josie is slowly working her way into Alix's life and into her home.
