Players take on the role of Sherlock Holmes's Baker Street Irregulars, roaming the streets of Victorian London with a map, a directory, and the day's newspaper, trying to solve ten new cases before Holmes himself does. This time, the investigations lead to some brand new locations, including murders at the luxurious Carlton House and the occasional crime scene in beautiful Queen's Park, along with two classic, long out-of-print expansions that have been revised and updated. Whether playing solo or with up to eight people, the challenge is to follow the clues, piece together the deductions, and come up with a solution that can stand up against Holmes's own.
