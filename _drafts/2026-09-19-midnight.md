In the third book of this series, Adrian Ring, a former Marine, is still writing in his diary as he tries to survive a world overrun by the undead. Holed up in the fortified private school in New Hampshire that he has made his home, he records his day-to-day struggle to stay alive, find supplies, and hold on to his sanity as he deals with both the dead and the living.
