Hadrian Marlowe is remembered across the galaxy as both a hero who burned every last alien Cielcin from the sky and a monster who destroyed a sun, killing four billion people, including the Emperor himself. But it all starts when a young Hadrian flees his father and a future as a torturer, only to end up stranded on a strange, backwater world. There, he is forced to fight as a gladiator and is drawn into the intrigues of a foreign planetary court, setting him on the path to a war he did not start, for an Empire he does not love, against an enemy he will never understand.
