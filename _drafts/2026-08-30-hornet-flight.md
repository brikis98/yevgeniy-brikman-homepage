It is June 1941, the war is going badly for England, and across the North Sea, 18-year-old Harald Olufsen takes a shortcut on the German-occupied Danish island of Sande and stumbles onto a discovery that could change the course of the war. He has to get word to England, but he has no way of getting there. All he has is an old, derelict Hornet Moth biplane rusting away in a ruined church, a plane so decrepit it may never get off the ground, even if Harald knew how to fly it.
