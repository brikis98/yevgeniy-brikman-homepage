After two years of wrong turns, defense attorney Mickey Haller is finally getting back into the courtroom. When Hollywood lawyer Jerry Vincent is murdered, Haller inherits his caseload, including the biggest case of his career: the defense of Walter Elliott, a prominent studio executive accused of murdering his wife and her lover. But as Haller prepares for trial, he learns that Vincent's killer may be coming for him next, and LAPD detective Harry Bosch, determined to find that killer, isn't above using Haller as bait.
