In the 21st book of this series, LAPD Detective Renée Ballard is working "the late show," the night beat out of Hollywood Station, when she returns in the early hours to find a stranger rifling through old file cabinets. The intruder is retired detective Harry Bosch, who is working a cold case that has gotten under his skin: the unsolved murder of Daisy Clayton, a fifteen-year-old runaway on the streets of Hollywood whose body was dumped in a dumpster. Ballard can't let him go through department records, but after he leaves, she looks into the case herself, and soon the two of them join forces to find out what happened to Daisy.
