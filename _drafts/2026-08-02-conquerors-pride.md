When a Commonwealth patrol makes first contact with an unknown alien race, the aliens open fire without warning and destroy every ship but one. Commander Pheylan Cavanagh, son of the powerful former politician Lord Stewart Cavanagh, survives the ambush only to be taken prisoner by the aliens. While the Commonwealth scrambles to prepare for a war against an enemy it knows almost nothing about, the elder Cavanagh decides to take matters into his own hands and get his son back.
