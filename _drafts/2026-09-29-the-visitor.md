Sergeant Amy Callan and Lieutenant Caroline Cook were both army high-flyers, both knew Jack Reacher, and both were forced to resign from the service. Now they're both dead, each found naked in her own home, in a bath full of paint. The apparent killer is an army man, a smart loner with a score to settle, a man just like Jack Reacher.
