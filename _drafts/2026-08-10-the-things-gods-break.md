In the second book of this series, Lyra Keres, a cursed mortal who has already made the mistake of mouthing off to the gods and falling for Hades, King of the Underworld, finds herself trapped in Tartarus, the deepest pit of the underworld. There, she must survive a series of horrifying trials against monsters worse than the gods, while the Titans, twisted by centuries of rage, wait behind seven ancient locks. The problem is that Lyra turns out to be the key to those locks, so if she breaks free, so do the Titans.
