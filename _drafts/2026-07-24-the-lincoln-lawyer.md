Mickey Haller is a criminal defense attorney who works out of the back seat of his Lincoln Town Car, driving between the far-flung courthouses of Los Angeles to defend bikers, con artists, drunk drivers, and drug dealers. For him, the law is rarely about guilt or innocence, and mostly about negotiation and manipulation. Then a Beverly Hills playboy arrested for attacking a woman he picked up in a bar hires Haller to defend him, giving Mickey his first high-paying client in years, and what looks like it might be the easiest case of his career.
