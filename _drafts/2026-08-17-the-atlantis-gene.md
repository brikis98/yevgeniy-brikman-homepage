Dr. Kate Warner is a geneticist in Jakarta, Indonesia, working on a breakthrough treatment for autism. David Vale is a counterterrorism agent who has been tracking a shadowy organization called Immari International. When a mysterious structure is discovered buried in the ice off Antarctica, and two of Kate's young patients are abducted from her lab, the two of them are pulled into a conspiracy that reaches back to the origins of humanity itself.
