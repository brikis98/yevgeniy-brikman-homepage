Joe works at a bookstore in New York, and when Guinevere Beck, an aspiring writer and recent Brown graduate, walks in one day, he's instantly smitten. Beck is everything Joe has ever wanted, and he'll stop at nothing to have her, so he starts to insinuate himself into every corner of her life: her friendships, her email, her phone. Then her boyfriend, Benji, mysteriously disappears, and Beck and Joe fall into a tumultuous affair.
