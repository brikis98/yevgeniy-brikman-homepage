The world-famous archaeologist Professor Ian Jones has recently passed away, leaving behind a lifetime of travels to every corner of the globe in search of lost treasures, secret codes, and forgotten writings. He loved puzzles, so naturally, finding his estate means solving one last riddle he left behind. You and up to three other players race against the clock, using what you learned from the professor and a set of tools that must be folded, written on, and torn apart along the way.
