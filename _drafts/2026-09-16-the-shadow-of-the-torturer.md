Severian is a young apprentice in the Guild of Torturers, raised within the guild's ancient tower in the city of Nessus on a far-future world called Urth. When he commits the ultimate sin of his profession, showing mercy toward one of his victims, he is exiled from the guild and sent on a journey out of Nessus and into the strange world beyond.
