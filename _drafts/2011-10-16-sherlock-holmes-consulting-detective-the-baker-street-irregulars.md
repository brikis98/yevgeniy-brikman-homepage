In 1880, Sherlock Holmes recruits a band of London street children as the Baker Street Irregulars, his unofficial "police force." Over the course of one eventful year, the Irregulars and the master detective are drawn into ten challenging and dangerous cases, from a kidnapping on Curzon Street to a heist in Harp Lane, and you have to walk the foggy streets of London in search of clues, piece together what happened, and then compare your deductions to those of Holmes himself.
