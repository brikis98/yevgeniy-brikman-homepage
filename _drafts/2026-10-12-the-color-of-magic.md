On the Discworld, a flat world carried through space on the backs of four elephants standing on a giant turtle, Rincewind is an inept and cowardly wizard scraping by in the city of Ankh-Morpork. When Twoflower, the Disc's first-ever tourist, arrives with a pocketful of gold and a luggage chest that follows him around on hundreds of little legs, Rincewind ends up hired as his guide. Soon the two are fleeing from one disaster to the next, running into dragons that only exist if you believe in them, and heading all the way to the edge of the world.
