In September 1938, Europe is on the brink of war as British Prime Minister Neville Chamberlain flies to Munich for a last-ditch conference with Adolf Hitler over the fate of Czechoslovakia. Among Chamberlain's staff is Hugh Legat, a young private secretary at 10 Downing Street, while in Berlin, Paul von Hartmann, a German diplomat and Legat's old friend from their days at Oxford, is secretly working with a group that opposes Hitler. When Hartmann obtains a secret document revealing Hitler's true plans for war, the two former friends find themselves drawn together in Munich, where Hartmann hopes to get it into British hands before the conference is over.
