In the second book of this series, Hadrian Marlowe has spent half a century searching the farther suns for the lost planet of Vorgossos, hoping it holds a way to contact the elusive alien Cielcin and end nearly four hundred years of war. After years chasing false leads among the barbarian Normans as captain of a band of mercenaries, he ventures beyond the security of the Sollan Empire and among the Extrasolarians who dwell between the stars. There, he must contend with creatures that were once human, traitors in his own ranks, and the aliens he has come to offer peace.
