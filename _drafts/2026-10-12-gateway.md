Robinette Broadhead is a young man stuck working in the food mines of a grim, overcrowded future Earth until he wins the lottery and uses the money to buy passage to Gateway, an asteroid left behind by a long-vanished alien race called the Heechee. Gateway is full of Heechee ships that still work, but no one understands how to steer them, so prospectors climb aboard, launch to unknown preset destinations, and hope they come back rich instead of dead. Years later, a wealthy and deeply troubled Rob recounts his time on Gateway in sessions with a robot psychiatrist he calls Sigfrid von Shrink, trying to come to terms with whatever happened out there.
