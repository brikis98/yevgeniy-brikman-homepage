In 1984, brothers Ron and Dan Lafferty, two Mormon fundamentalists, murder their sister-in-law and her baby daughter, insisting they received a revelation from God commanding them to do it. Starting from this "divinely inspired" crime, the book looks into the isolated communities across the American West, Canada, and Mexico where some forty thousand Mormon fundamentalists still practice polygamy, defying both civil authorities and the mainstream church in Salt Lake City. Along the way, it digs into Mormonism's violent history and asks hard questions about the nature of religious belief and where faith ends and extremism begins.
