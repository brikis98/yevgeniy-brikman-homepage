Amid St. Mark's Square, the Grand Canal, and the Rialto Bridge, a team of players is sent to Venice on a top-secret mission to uncover a mysterious conspiracy and save the city. Modeled after an escape room, the game has players work together to solve a chain of puzzles, using components that must be folded, written on, and torn apart along the way, so it can only be played once.
