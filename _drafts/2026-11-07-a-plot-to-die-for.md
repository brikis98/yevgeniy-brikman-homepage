Finn O'Leary is a celebrity gardener who returns to his hometown of Abbeyford, Ireland, to care for his ageing mother, and quickly gets roped into the local Tidy Towns committee, where his best friend's sister, Aoife, is determined that this will finally be Abbeyford's year. But Finn has barely unpacked when an alto-baritone drops dead during his mother's choir practice, midway through a rendition of 'What the World Needs Now'. So, with his mother, her carer Happiness, and Aoife in tow, Finn sets out to discover who has brought murder to Abbeyford.
