Melanie is a ten-year-old girl who loves school, and whom Dr. Caldwell calls "our little genius." Every morning, she waits in her cell to be collected for class, and when they come for her, Sergeant keeps his gun pointed at her while two of his people strap her into a wheelchair. She jokes that she won't bite, but no one laughs, and she doesn't understand why her favorite teacher, Miss Justineau, looks so sad whenever Melanie talks about what she'll do when she grows up.
