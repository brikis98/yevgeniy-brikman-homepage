Times are rough, and life in your village keeps getting harder, until seafarers start telling stories about Catan, a beautiful, fertile island with lush vegetation and a wide variety of resources. Hoping for better times, you and a few friends set off to explore this new world and settle there. But the journey is full of challenges and mysteries, and you'll have to solve them, trade with the residents, and build out the island if you want to make a new life in a foreign land.
