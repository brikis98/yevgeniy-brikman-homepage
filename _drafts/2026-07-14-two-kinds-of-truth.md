In the 20th book of this series, Harry Bosch, exiled from the LAPD, is working cold cases for the tiny San Fernando Police Department when two pharmacists are murdered in a robbery at a local drugstore, and the trail leads him undercover into the dangerous world of pill mills and prescription drug abuse. Meanwhile, a killer Bosch put away decades ago claims Harry framed him, and seems to have new evidence to prove it. With his former LAPD colleagues not keen to protect his reputation, Bosch must clear his name on his own, or risk every case he ever worked being called into question.
