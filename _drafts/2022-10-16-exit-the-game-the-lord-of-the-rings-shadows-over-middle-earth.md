A threat is growing in Mordor, where the shadows loom, and Frodo and his companions have set out to save Middle-earth. This time, though, they need help: the wizard Gandalf sends you and your fellow players on a crucial mission, and if you fail to complete his assignments, all hope dies. Together, you visit locations from The Lord of the Rings, solve tricky magic puzzles, and support the companions against Nazgûl, orcs, and other dark figures.
