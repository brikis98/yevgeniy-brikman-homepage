A group of travelers takes shelter for the night in an abandoned cabin, but when morning comes, they find the door secured by a combination lock that no one knows the code to, and the windows barred shut. All they have to go on is a mysterious book and an enigmatic spinning code dial. To earn their freedom, the players must work together to crack codes, solve puzzles, and collect objects, bit by bit, before they can escape.
