One winter evening, Elín S. Jónsdóttir, a bestselling crime author, goes missing without a trace. With no clues to go on, it's up to Helgi, a young detective, to solve the case before the press gets wind of it. As he interviews the people closest to her, including her publisher, her accountant, and a retired judge, he begins to realize that Elín's life wasn't what it seemed, and that her past may be even stranger than the fiction she wrote.
