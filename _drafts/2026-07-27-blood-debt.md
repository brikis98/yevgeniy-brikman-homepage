In the 11th book of this series, Victor is back: a ruthless, meticulous, and highly paranoid professional assassin who lives entirely off the grid, trusts no one, and survives by planning for every possible threat. As always, his line of work means that enemies from his violent past are never far behind, and keeping himself alive means staying one step ahead of everyone who wants him dead.
