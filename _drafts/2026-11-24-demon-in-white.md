In the third book of this series, Hadrian Marlowe has spent almost a hundred years fighting for the Empire against the Cielcin, a vicious alien race bent on humanity's destruction. Now there are rumors of a new Cielcin king who doesn't raid the borderworlds like the others, but instead launches precise, strategic attacks on the Empire, while back home, legends that Hadrian defied death have turned him into the "Halfmortal" and made him enemies within the Imperial government who resent his growing influence. And above it all is the mystery of the Quiet, and the only place in the universe that might hold the answers Hadrian seeks is controlled by the Emperor himself.

## Notes

- It's interesting how they keep armies of legions asleep in Cryo sleep until they're necessary
- The time scale in the book are crazy. People living for hundreds of years, but also going into deep freeze so they can even be around for thousands of years. Wars that last many many centuries. Planning and strategies that take 70 years to execute.
- Badass battles. 
  
  Stopping high matter with his adamant hand. 
  
  Robot alien hybrids. 
  
  Giant robots. 
  
  Space ships.
- The multiverse like switching of Adrienne’s is cool.
