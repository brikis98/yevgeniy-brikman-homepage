In the third book of this series, President Gwen Iceni and General Artur Drakon have liberated the Midway Star System from Syndicate rule, but the Syndicate isn't about to let it go: its leaders have ordered the rebels terminated and Midway's citizens punished, and they're assembling a fleet that badly outnumbers Midway's warships. Meanwhile, a former ally has seized control of the Ulindi Star System as a first step toward building his own empire. And as violence erupts on Midway itself, Iceni and Drakon must face a renewed revolt, along with trusted officers who may be waiting for the chance to betray them.
