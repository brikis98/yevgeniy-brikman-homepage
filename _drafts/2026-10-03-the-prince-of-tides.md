Tom Wingo is an out-of-work high school teacher and football coach from the South Carolina low country whose marriage is falling apart. When his twin sister Savannah, a gifted poet living in New York City, attempts suicide, Tom travels north and meets her psychiatrist, Dr. Susan Lowenstein. To help her treat Savannah, Tom begins telling her the story of their family, revealing a dark and tragic legacy that spans forty years.
