It's Victorian London, and you're one of the Baker Street Irregulars, working alongside Sherlock Holmes to crack a series of baffling cases: two lions murdered in Hyde Park, paintings gone missing from the National Gallery, and the killing of a man named Oswald Mason. With no dice and no luck involved, you must trace the threads of evidence yourself, interviewing suspects across the city, combing through the day's newspapers for clues, and piecing together the facts. Once you think you've reached a solution, you can compare your deductions with how the master sleuth himself would have solved the case.
