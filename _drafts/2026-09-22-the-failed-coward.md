In the fourth book of this series, Adrian Ring and his adopted family at Auburn Lake begin to realize that the dreams they've been having since the zombie apocalypse began are more than just nightmares, and that Adrian may be at the center of a growing darkness. His friend Kevin, though stationed overseas, seems somehow connected to it too. Then a thousand mindless dead show up at the lake holding books, and it looks a lot like someone is trying to send a message from beyond the grave.
