In 1921 Texas, Elsa Wolcott is considered too old to marry, until one night with Rafe Martinelli ruins her reputation and leaves her with only one respectable choice: marrying a man she barely knows. By 1934, the Great Depression and a devastating drought have turned the Great Plains into a dust bowl, and everything on the Martinelli farm is dying, including Elsa's fragile marriage. As she fights each day to keep her children alive, Elsa faces an agonizing choice: stay and fight for the land she loves, or leave it all behind and head west to California in search of a better life.
