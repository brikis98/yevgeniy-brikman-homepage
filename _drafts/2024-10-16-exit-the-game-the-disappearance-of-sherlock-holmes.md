Sherlock Holmes stands accused of murdering the writer Ignatius Loyde, and the famous detective has gone into hiding. Working alongside Dr. Watson, the players must follow the trail of clues, solving riddle after riddle to figure out how Holmes became the prime suspect, what role Moriarty plays in it all, and how to prove Holmes' innocence.
