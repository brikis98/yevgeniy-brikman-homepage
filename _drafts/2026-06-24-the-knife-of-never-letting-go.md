The Knife of Never Letting Go is a 2008 young adult science fiction novel by Patrick Ness and the first book in the Chaos Walking trilogy. It is set on a colonized planet where a condition called the Noise makes everyone's thoughts audible to those around them. The story follows Todd Hewitt, a boy approaching manhood in Prentisstown, a settlement with no women, who discovers something that forces him to flee his home.
