In the 16th book of this series, Inspector Gamache and his family are in Paris, where they gather for a bistro dinner with Armand's godfather, the elderly billionaire Stephen Horowitz. Walking home after the meal, they watch in horror as Stephen is struck down and critically injured in what Gamache knows is no accident, but a deliberate attempt on his life. When a strange key turns up in Stephen's possession, Gamache, his wife Reine-Marie, and his former second-in-command, Jean-Guy Beauvoir, set out to uncover the secrets his godfather has kept for decades.
