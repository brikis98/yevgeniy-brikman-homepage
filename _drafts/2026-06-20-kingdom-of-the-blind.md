Kingdom of the Blind is a 2018 mystery novel by Louise Penny and the fourteenth book in her series featuring Armand Gamache of the Sûreté du Québec. While suspended from duty, Gamache is unexpectedly named as an executor of the will of an elderly woman he never met, alongside bookseller Myrna Landers and a young builder.
