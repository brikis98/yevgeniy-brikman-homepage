Kingdom of the Blind is a 2018 mystery novel by Canadian author Louise Penny, the fourteenth book in her series featuring Armand Gamache of the Sûreté du Québec. In it, Gamache is named an executor of a stranger's will, a request that draws him into an unusual inheritance and its consequences. The story is set partly in the series' recurring village of Three Pines in Quebec.
